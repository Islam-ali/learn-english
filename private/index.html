                                              Task 02 · Vocabulary

                                              What's the weather like?

                                              Tap a word, then tap the picture it goes with. Then check your answers and listen to each sentence.

                                              Say them right

                                              Tap a word to hear it. In the IPA, the mark ˈ comes before the stressed syllable.

                                              It's sunny / cloudy / windy / foggy / hot / cold = It's + adjective. It's raining / snowing = present continuous: it is happening now. For a general fact, use the simple present: It rains a lot in spring. It snows in winter.

                                              Hot, warm, cool or cold?

                                              Move the slider. Warm is close to hot (a nice temperature). Cool is close to cold. New York uses °F, so you see both.

                                              coldcoolwarmhot
                                              22°C 72°F It's warm.

                                              The four seasons

                                              Which season is each photo? Tap a name.

                                              In American English, fall /fɔːl/. In British English, autumn /ˈɔːtəm/. Both are correct. We say in the summer, in winter.
